# Praylist Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 11, 2026  
Operator: VXDeveloper  
Contact: visionexperiencedeveloper@gmail.com

Praylist is an app for writing down your prays and recording prayer and when your prays are answered. This policy applies to the processing of personal information in the Praylist iPhone app.

## 1. The app does not collect or transmit personal information

Praylist does not require an account and does not transmit the prays or prayer records you create to developer servers. It does not use advertising, behavioral analytics tools, advertising tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. The developer cannot access the notebook stored on your device.

## 2. Information stored on your device

- Category names, descriptions, symbols, and order
- pray content, saved notes, creation dates, achievement status, and achievement dates
- Dates on which you recorded a prayer
- Whether reminders are enabled and the reminder time
- App language preference

This information is used to display your notebook, edit content, record prayers, and provide reminder and language settings. The prayer calendar displays dates stored in the app and does not access events in your iPhone's Calendar.

## 3. Language and region settings

When automatic language selection is enabled, the app displays Korean if your iPhone's Region setting is South Korea, and English otherwise. You can also choose Korean or English directly in the app's settings.

The app does not collect GPS or your physical location to select a language and does not request location permission. The Region setting is checked only on your device. Changing the language does not automatically translate or alter the pray or category content you have written.

## 4. Prayer reminders

The app requests iOS notification permission only when you enable prayer reminders. Notifications are scheduled on your device for the time you choose and do not include the pray content you have written.

You can change notification permission at any time in your iPhone's Settings. You can continue to write prays, record prayers, and use the app's other features without allowing notifications.

## 5. Backups and restoration

When you export a backup, the app creates a JSON file containing your categories, prays, prayer records, and reminder settings. You choose where to save the file and whom to share it with. Language preferences are managed separately on each device.

Backup files contain personal prays and records, and Praylist does not add a separate password to exported JSON files. Please take care when storing or sharing them. Files you save to an external service such as iCloud Drive or share with another person are subject to that service's policies and the sharing settings you choose.

Praylist does not provide automatic synchronization through its own servers. However, depending on your iPhone backup settings, app data may be included in device backups provided by Apple or another backup environment you use.

## 6. Retention and deletion

Records remain on your device until you delete them. You can delete prays or categories in the app. Deleting the app also removes the records in the app's storage on that device.

Files you have exported separately and copies included in operating system backups are not removed simply by deleting the app. Manage or delete those files and backups separately at their storage location. The developer cannot recover or delete records stored only on your device on your behalf.

## 7. Information you provide when contacting us

If you contact us by email, we use your email address, message, and any materials you choose to attach to respond to your inquiry. Your prays and backup files are not attached automatically. You do not need to send personal prays or sensitive information that is unnecessary for your inquiry.

We retain inquiry information to resolve the inquiry and provide necessary follow-up, and delete it when it is no longer needed. If applicable law requires retention, the information may be retained for the required period. The email provider's policies apply to its processing of email.
