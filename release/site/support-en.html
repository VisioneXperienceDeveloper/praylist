Praylist Support

Keep your prays close. Make time to pray.

Praylist is an iPhone app for organizing your prays into categories and recording prayer and the dates your prays were answered.

How do I create my first pray?

When you first open the app, choose the categories you want and write your first pray. After that, tap an empty line in your notebook, enter your text, and tap Done on the keyboard to save. Tap the text of an existing pray to edit it in place.

How many prays can a category hold?

Each category holds up to 10 prays, including answered ones. When all the spaces are full, create another category or delete a pray you no longer need. Exporting a backup before deleting lets you keep a separate copy of your records.

How do I change my categories or their order?

Go to Settings → Manage categories to change a category's name, description, or symbol. Tap Edit to change the order. Swipe left or right in your notebook, or use the arrows below it, to view another category. At least one category must remain.

How do I change the language?

Go to Settings → Language and select Use device region, 한국어, or English. Use device region automatically displays Korean when your iPhone's Region setting is South Korea and English otherwise. This does not check your physical location or GPS and does not request location permission.

Your manual language choice is remembered the next time you open the app. Changing the language does not translate or alter prays or category content you have already written.

How do Today's prayer and prayer records work?

Tap Today's prayer below your notebook to read your prays. At the end, tap I prayed today to record that day's prayer. Recording more than once on the same day still marks just one day on the calendar.

Open My journey at the top of the notebook and select Prayer calendar to see a dot on each recorded day. This calendar displays only your app records and does not read or change events in your iPhone's Calendar.

How do I record answered prays and achievement dates?

Tap the circle beside a pray to set its achievement date. Answered prays remain in your notebook and are also gathered in My journey. Tap the circle again to change the date, mark the pray as unanswered, or delete it.

How do I turn on prayer reminders?

Go to Settings → Daily prayer reminder, enable reminders, choose a time, and save. When you first use reminders, allow iOS notification permission. The app schedules a daily notification on your device at your chosen time. Personal pray content is never included in the notification.

Tap the notification to open the app and Today's prayer. Opening the notification alone does not record a prayer. Tap I prayed today after you finish.

Why am I not receiving reminders?

Check that notifications are allowed in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Praylist. Then check the reminder time and enabled state in the app and save them again. Focus, Scheduled Summary, and sound settings can affect how notifications are delivered. Reminders follow local time when your device's time zone changes. The app's other features work without notifications.

How do I move my records to a new device?

On your existing device, go to Settings → Export a backup and save the JSON file. On your new device, go to Settings → Restore a backup and select that file. Restoring replaces your current notebook with the backup's contents, so first export any current records you want to keep.

After restoring, turn on prayer reminders again on that device. Language preferences are kept separately on each device. Praylist does not provide automatic synchronization through its own servers.

Backup files contain your prays and records and do not have a separate password. Choose their storage location and recipients yourself, and keep them safe. Device backups controlled by iOS settings are separate from manual JSON exports.

How do I delete my records?

Tap the circle beside a pray to delete that pray. Deleting a category in Settings → Manage categories also deletes the prays it contains. To remove all the app records stored on a device, delete the app from your iPhone.

Separately exported files and device backups may remain after you delete the app, so manage them at their storage location. The developer cannot recover records stored only on your device on your behalf.
