Make time for your prays.

Praylist is a quiet place for your bucket list and daily prayer. Keep your prays together and return to them each day.

Who would you like to become? What would you like to have, try, or experience? Choose the categories that matter to you and write your first pray. No account needed.

Ten prays, one page
Keep up to 10 prays in each category, including answered ones. Swipe between pages to see what you hold close. Tap an empty line to write, or tap your words to edit in place.

CATEGORIES THAT FEEL LIKE YOU
Start with suggested categories, then add your own names, descriptions, and symbols. Arrange them in the order you prefer.

A DAILY MOMENT OF PRAYER
Set an optional daily reminder. Open the notification to go straight to today's prayer. Read your prays in your own words and record the day when you finish.

REMEMBER WHAT CAME TRUE
Mark a pray as answered and save its achievement date. My journey brings answered prays together, with a calendar marking the days you prayed.

PERSONAL BY DESIGN
Your notebook stays on your device. No account, ads, analytics, or developer server. Export a backup to Files and restore it when you need to.

YOUR LANGUAGE
Use Korean or English. Choose your language in Settings. Automatic selection uses Korean when your iPhone Region is Korea and English elsewhere. Your own writing is never automatically translated.
